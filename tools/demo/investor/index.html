What a brand buys

Bike-hours in the areas and hours it picks. Each bike-hour promises 200 ads. If the road falls short, the system makes up the plays, then refunds what it could not deliver. People counts are estimates and are never billed.

Why the counts can be trusted

The screen signs every ad it plays, like a receipt. The cloud only counts signed plays from fitted screens, never twice, and never invents one.

What this is, and is not

The bikes are simulated; the roads, the areas and the software are real. It shows the system keeps up and delivers. It does not predict sales. Real numbers come from the 200-bike test fleet.
