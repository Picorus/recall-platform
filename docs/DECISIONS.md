| 25 Oct 2026 | Screen line, cloud side (P2.3.2): EMQX takes screens only over mutual TLS with our screen certificates; the gateway decides every connection (certificate issued to this screen, in date, not revoked, screen not reported lost) and every topic (own topics only), refusing anything else or on any error. The certificate is identified by its MD5 fingerprint (EMQX cannot pass the whole certificate safely; the TLS handshake already proves the key). Each message and subscription is checked again, so a box revoked or reported lost is cut off at its next message. Per-box limits (5 messages/s, 512 KB/s), no retained messages, strong ciphers only, cluster ports local, secrets at least 32 characters. | 25 Oct 2026 | Screen line (P2.3.1): each screen sends only on `recall/v1/s/<id>/up/<kind>` and listens only on `…/down/<kind>`.
