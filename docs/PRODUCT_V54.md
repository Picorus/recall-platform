# RECALL v5.4 — Simple product: full bike-hours (decided 21 Oct 2026)

No change to model numbers. v5.3 already assumes one bike-hour = 240 spots of 15 s = 200 brand plays + 40 RECALL spots, priced Peak ₹300 / Normal ₹250 / Lite ₹220 per bike-hour (= 10 × the old share-hour price of ₹30/₹25/₹22). The only change is HOW it is sold: one brand takes the whole 200 plays on a bike for an hour. Cash, profit, break-even, funding: all unchanged (v5.3).

## Positioning
- RECALL is an alternative to offline (hoardings, pamphlets, newspaper inserts), not an online ad platform.
- Keep it simple. The more we give, the more they expect. We already give far more than a hoarding can.

## What we sell
- Full bike-hours only. No shared screens: on a bike, in that hour, only that brand's ads play.
- One bike-hour = 200 plays of the brand's 15 s ads. The other 40 spots are RECALL's (our ads + buffer).
- Prices per bike-hour (unchanged): Peak ₹300 (08–11, 17–20, 20–23), Normal ₹250 (11–17), Lite ₹220 (23–02). Plus 18% GST.
- Brand picks: area(s) → hour(s) → days → number of bikes (minimum 5 bikes per booking). Quick packs such as "5 evenings" offered as options.
- Minimum spend ₹15,000 per booking. Quick picks ₹15,000 / ₹50,000 / ₹3 L+ / custom.
- Offers only for longer commitments: 6 months 8% off, 12 months 12% off; never below ₹220 a bike-hour (₹22 a share floor).

## The promise (only this)
- Example: Madhapur, Tue 5–6 pm, 20 bikes → 20 × 200 = 4,000 plays inside Madhapur between 5 and 6 pm, on at least 20 different bikes.
- Bikes move in and out of an area. Any RECALL bike inside the area serves that area's bookings; a bike that crosses from Madhapur into Kondapur starts serving Kondapur's bookings.
- Up to 10% of plays may fall just outside the boundary. Fully outside the area, the screen plays RECALL's own ads.
- If short: made up in the next 2 hours in that area; else the same hour next day in that area; else refund.
- Disclaimer shown at booking with a tick box.
- We do not promise a number of bikes at any moment, and we give no estimates of bikes per area.

## Areas
- Areas are defined by RECALL, geofenced on the real map (e.g. Madhapur, Kondapur). Not drawn around a brand's outlet.

## Money
- 100% paid in advance (UPI / card / bank). Longer commitments: each month paid before it starts.
- No refund if the brand does not upload ads on time; they can talk to support.

## Ads (creatives)
- RECALL does not make ads. Fixed templates with limits (logo, headline with character limit, offer line, optional QR / coupon code, colour themes) so upload is easy.
- Minimum 2, maximum 5 ads per booking; they take turns so people at a signal are not bored. One tap auto-creates a second version (colour or offer line swapped) for brands with one ad.
- We show best practices; we only suggest, it is their choice.
- Coupon codes and QR allowed on the ad. RECALL does not track them.
- Ads checked only for rules (no flashing, nothing like a road sign, no banned categories).

## Reporting
- No daily updates during the campaign. Two messages only: "booked" and "done + report".
- One report after the campaign ends: plays delivered, number of different bikes, heat map of areas covered, estimated head count (camera, shown as a rounded range, marked approximate; never billed or guaranteed).
- No photos. (Dropped. If ever needed later, it is the account manager's job, by hand.)
- No business analytics (no code/QR tracking, no cost per customer, no ROI).

## Free trial (unchanged, simplified)
- One per business, 1 week, 1 area, about 20 bike-hours (≈ ₹5,000), from RECALL spots left after make-ups. Nothing charged. Same final report, no photos.

## Removed from earlier ideas
- Shared slots / share-hours sold to brands (the word "share-hour" stays only inside the model as 1/10 of a bike-hour).
- Outlet maps and radius around a brand's shop; bike estimates per area.
- Ad-making service ("made for you"), refunds for late ads.
- Live campaign page, daily alerts, street photos, code/QR tracking, results analytics, AutoPay mandates.
- 80% sell cap (not used; the 40 RECALL spots per bike-hour are the buffer). Watch weekly refunds as the warning sign of overselling.

## Portal (brand sees 9 screens)
1 Sign in · 2 Your business (name, GST, contact) · 3 My campaigns (Scheduled / Running / Done) · 4 Book (area, hours, days, bikes, price) · 5 Your ads (templates, 2–5 ads, best practices) · 6 Review and pay (full advance, disclaimers) · 7 Final report · 8 Invoices · 9 Help and account.
