The city at night, on screen.

A new look for the rider app, the brand portal and the staff portal. Same colours, made calmer and richer. Every screen below is a working prototype: tap it, click it, try the keyboard.

Rider app like Uber DriverBrand portal like AirbnbStaff portal like Stripe
Three apps, three jobs

Each one designed for how it is used.

A rider glances at the phone at a red light. A brand owner plans a campaign on a laptop over coffee. Staff work through a queue all day. So the three apps share one family look, but each is built around its moment.

RIDER APP · DARK · ONE THUMB

Readable in one second, at a signal.

The map fills the screen. Today’s money sits on top, the screen’s health in a sheet below. Nothing to read in full sentences while moving.

  • Four tabs: Home, Earnings, Screen, Account
  • Big numbers, big touch targets (48px+)
  • తెలుగు and हिन्दी on every screen
BRAND PORTAL · LIGHT · PHOTO-LED

Areas are places you book.

Like choosing a stay: pick an area from real night pictures, see when people are there, set dates and bikes, and the price is always on screen.

  • One search bar: area · dates · hours · bikes
  • Live price card that never leaves the screen
  • Campaigns you can watch running
STAFF PORTAL · LIGHT · KEYBOARD FIRST

Calm, dense, and fast to move around.

A side menu grouped by job, a search box that jumps anywhere (Ctrl K), tables that open details beside them so you never lose your place.

  • “Needs you” list on the home page
  • Filters as chips, details in a side panel
  • Menus still filtered by role

05 · Motion

Quick to answer, slow to settle.

Motion only explains what happened: a press, a change, where something came from. Things answer a tap in a tenth of a second and settle softly, never bounce for show. Phones set to “reduce motion” get none of it.

06 · Pictures

Hyderabad after dark, made for us.

No stock photos. I drew these night scenes with code, in the style of long-exposure street photography: wet roads, tail-light trails, sodium lamps, the glass of Hitech City and the cable bridge over Durgam Cheruvu. They are ours to use anywhere, and when you have real photos of our bikes they slot into the same places.

07 · What happens next

Built in five steps, each one safe.

The money, plays and rules underneath do not change; only how they look and feel. Every step is its own change with all tests passing, so you can stop after any step.

STEP 1The shared kitColours, type, buttons, forms, tables, icons and motion, built once for all three apps.
STEP 2Brand portalAreas as places, the booking card, campaigns and reports. What customers judge us by.
STEP 3Rider appMap home, earnings, screen, account, Telugu and Hindi, daylight mode.
STEP 4Staff portalSide menu, Ctrl K, tables with side panels, the new home page.
STEP 5Polish and proofEvery page at phone and laptop size, new screenshots, guides updated.
