# Staff portal: a guide

The staff portal is where RECALL's own team runs the business. It covers campaigns, bikes and screens, riders, ads, money and reports.

In the demo, open it from the **demo desk** → **Open the staff portal**.

## The menu, page by page

The menu runs down the left, grouped by job: Sales, Fleet, Work, Money and Admin. Press **Ctrl K** (⌘K on a Mac), or click **Jump to…**, to type a page's name and go straight there. Here is what each page is for.

### Today
![Today](img/staff-today.png)

The morning page. It shows:
- campaigns running now;
- bike-hours sold today, and how many have been delivered so far;
- what needs a person, such as ads to review, refunds waiting or screens that are silent.

**Open this first in a demo.**

### Dashboard
![Dashboard](img/staff-dashboard.png)

The business in numbers: sales, delivery, fleet health and money, over days and weeks.

### Fleet map
![Fleet map](img/staff-fleet.png)

Every bike on a live map of Hyderabad, with a coloured dot for each screen:
- moving;
- stopped;
- silent.

It refreshes by itself. With 2,000 bikes it looks like this:

![Fleet map at 2,000 bikes](img/staff-fleet-2000.png)

### Screens
![Screens](img/staff-screens.png)

Every screen. The tiles at the top count screens:
- on bikes;
- spares in stock;
- in repair;
- retired.

The table shows each screen's bike, whether it is moving, its player version and its fitting check. Click **Manage** on a screen to:
- move it;
- send it for repair;
- retire it;
- report it lost or stolen. Its plays then stop counting from that moment.

### Areas
![Areas](img/staff-areas.png)

The parts of the city that brands can buy, such as Madhapur and Gachibowli. Click **Add an area** to add one. Changing an area makes a new version; old campaigns keep the old shape.

### Calendar
![Calendar](img/staff-calendar.png)

Campaigns by day and hour: which areas are sold, and how full each hour is.

### Brands
![Brands](img/staff-brands.png)

Every business and agency. **Open** a brand to see:
- its campaigns, contacts and tax details;
- its invoices.

Staff can also **draft a campaign** for a brand here and send it a payment link.

### Prices
![Prices](img/staff-prices.png)

The price for each hour of the day, per bike-hour, before GST. There are three bands:
- Peak: ₹300;
- Normal: ₹250;
- Lite: ₹220.

The page also holds the floor price, minimums and offers.

Saving makes a **new version** that starts at a time you choose. Campaigns already booked keep the old price.

### Ad review
![Ad review](img/staff-reviews.png)

Every ad a brand uploads waits here until someone approves or refuses it. A screen plays only approved ads.

### Delivery
![Delivery](img/staff-delivery.png)

For each campaign, the plays promised against the plays delivered. If a campaign falls short:
- the system first tries to **make up** the missing plays;
- what can't be made up becomes a **refund**, which waits here for a person to approve.

### Riders
![Riders](img/staff-riders.png)

Every rider and where each one stands: applied, approved, fitted or riding. Here you can:
- search for a rider;
- see fitting slots;
- open a rider to check documents, ID and bank, and to pause them.

### Support
![Support](img/staff-support.png)

Conversations with brands and riders, from calls, WhatsApp or the apps. Each one shows who is handling it and where it stands. Click **New conversation** to log a call.

### Payouts
![Payouts](img/staff-payouts.png)

Riders are paid every Monday. The system prepares the run. Then a **second person** must approve it before any money goes out. In the demo, a run is waiting for approval.

### Money
![Money](img/staff-money.png)

The books. This page shows:
- money received from brands;
- GST owed;
- refunds;
- what is owed to riders.

Every rupee is recorded twice, once on each side, so the books always balance.

### Reports
![Reports](img/staff-reports.png)

The campaign reports sent to brands. **Open** one to see exactly what the brand saw.

### Staff
![Staff](img/staff-staff.png)

Who works at RECALL and what each person can open. Invite someone, change a role, or remove access.

### Audit log
![Audit log](img/staff-audit.png)

Every change anyone made: who, what, when, and the value before and after. Nothing is ever deleted; a mistake is reversed, and the reversal is logged too.

### Settings
![Settings](img/staff-settings.png)

Company details, fitting centres, support hours, and the other rules the business runs on.

## 5-minute demo script (for investors)

Sign in as the founder before the call starts.

| Time | Click | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Today** | "This is our control room. Today we sold about 900 bike-hours, and the system is tracking delivery live." |
| 0:45 | **Fleet map** | "Each dot is a bike with our screen, on real Hyderabad roads. Every 15 seconds a screen plays an ad and signs it, like a receipt we can't fake." |
| 1:30 | **Delivery** | "Brands pay for bike-hours. We promise 200 plays per bike-hour. If we fall short, the system first makes it up, then refunds automatically, so the brand only pays for what it got." |
| 2:15 | **Reports** → **Open** | "This is the report every brand gets: plays delivered, how many bikes, where the ads played. People counts are estimates, and we never bill for them." |
| 3:00 | **Money** | "Every rupee is recorded twice, once on each side, so the books always balance. GST invoices are automatic." |
| 3:30 | **Payouts** | "Riders are paid every Monday. One person prepares the run and a second person must approve it, so no single person can move money." |
| 4:00 | **Audit log** | "Nothing is deleted. Every change shows who made it, when, and what it was before." |
| 4:30 | **Riders** | "Riders join from their phone: ID check, bank check and the agreement, all done in the app." |

**If you have more time:** sign out and sign in as `ops@recall.demo`. The menu shrinks to operations only. This shows that staff see only what they need.
